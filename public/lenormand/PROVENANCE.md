# Petit Lenormand card artwork

- Source: *Das Spiel der Hofnung (The Game of Hope)*
- Creator: Johann Kaspar Hechtel
- Date: 1799
- Holding institution: The British Museum
- Digital source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Das_Spiel_der_Hofnung_(The_Game_of_Hope).png)
- Rights: Public domain / Public Domain Mark 1.0
- DIVINE treatment: the complete scan was cropped into its 36 individual cards and encoded as WebP. No imagery was redrawn or generatively extended.

The filenames preserve the original numbered sequence, which corresponds to the modern Petit Lenormand order from Rider (01) through Cross (36).
