# DIVINE astrology atlas provenance

These display images are grayscale, resized derivatives of public-domain
historical scans. The full downloaded source files are preserved in
`assets/astrology-source/`.

- `astrological-charts-1715.webp`: _Astrological charts_, engraving published
  in William Derham's _Astro-theology_ (1715), Library of Congress. No known
  restrictions on publication. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Astrological_charts_LCCN2002697697.jpg
- `zodiac-star-charts-1750.webp`: John Bevis, _12 star charts of the signs of
  the Zodiac_ (c. 1750). Public domain. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:12_star_charts_of_the_signs_of_the_Zodiac_by_John_Bevis.jpg
  The files in `signs/` are individual plate crops derived from this atlas.
- `zodiac-circle-medieval.webp`: zodiac circle with planets from National
  Library of Wales manuscript MS 735C, copied c. 1000. CC0. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F4.v._zodiac_circle_with_planets_-_NLW_MS_735C.png

## Background plates

The original downloaded files are preserved in `assets/astrology-source/`.
Optimized display derivatives live in `public/astrology/backgrounds/`. These
sources were selected for this personal, non-commercial project; retain the
source links and check the host's current terms before any broader reuse.

- `hevelius-sextans-1690.webp`: Johannes Hevelius, _Sextans Uraniae_ from
  _Firmamentum Sobiescianum_ (1690). Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sextans_Uraniae_(1690).jpg
- `hyginus-equus-1482.webp`: _Equus_, a constellation woodcut from the 1482
  edition of Hyginus's _Poeticon Astronomicon_. Source collection:
  https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/the-hyginus-star-atlas-1482/
- `al-sufi-fixed-stars-ca1430.webp`: paired constellation illustrations from
  al-Sufi's _Illustrated Book of Fixed Stars_, manuscript commissioned by
  Ulugh Beg around 1430. The BnF digital copy is marked for non-commercial
  use. Source collection:
  https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/fixed-stars/
- `astrological-man-15c.webp`: _Astrological man_, fifteenth-century
  manuscript, Royal Library Dresden / Wellcome Collection. CC BY 4.0 digital
  image. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Astrological_man._15th_century_manuscript._Wellcome_M0007101.jpg
- `uranias-mirror-1825.webp`: Sidney Hall, constellation card showing Cetus
  and neighboring figures from _Urania's Mirror_ (c. 1825). Source collection:
  https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/uranias-mirror-or-a-view-of-the-heavens/
- `aestas-1617.webp`: Jan van de Velde II, _Zomer (Aestas), met
  sterrenbeelden_ (1617), Rijksmuseum. Public domain. Source:
  https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/object/Zomer-Aestas-met-sterrenbeelden--177b07a14ae8a3b09f024186e87c783e
- `planetary-personifications-1464.webp`: Jupiter and Venus from an
  astronomical miscellany made shortly after 1464, J. Paul Getty Museum.
  `planetary-personification-detail-1464.webp` is an image-only display crop
  from the same plate.
  Source feature and image:
  https://www.getty.edu/news/written-in-the-stars-astronomy-and-astrology-in-medieval-manuscripts/
