# DIVINE astrology atlas provenance

These display images are grayscale, resized derivatives of public-domain
historical scans. The full downloaded source files are preserved in
`assets/astrology-source/`.

- `astrological-charts-1715.webp`: _Astrological charts_, engraving published
  in William Derham's _Astro-theology_ (1715), Library of Congress. No known
  restrictions on publication. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Astrological_charts_LCCN2002697697.jpg
- `zodiac-star-charts-1750.webp`: John Bevis, _12 star charts of the signs of
  the Zodiac_ (c. 1750). Public domain. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:12_star_charts_of_the_signs_of_the_Zodiac_by_John_Bevis.jpg
- `zodiac-circle-medieval.webp`: zodiac circle with planets from National
  Library of Wales manuscript MS 735C, copied c. 1000. CC0. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F4.v._zodiac_circle_with_planets_-_NLW_MS_735C.png
