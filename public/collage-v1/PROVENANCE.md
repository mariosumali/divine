# DIVINE Collage Library v1

Thirty original transparent-background collage assets generated for DIVINE with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-02.

The generation direction used the user's editorial collage references only for high-level spacing and atmosphere. The subjects and compositions are original: faded monochrome silver-gelatin photography crossed with soft photogravure ink, lifted blacks, dusty highlights, old-camera grain, restrained detail, centered archival flash lighting, and generous transparent margins. Prompts explicitly excluded text, logos, watermarks, frames, paper-craft styling, copied compositions, and clipped edges.

Assets: apple, bell, bust, compass, crystal, domino, door, envelope, eye, feather, glove, hand, heart, hourglass, key, mask, matches, moth, pearl, pen, peony, pomegranate, prism, ribbon, rose, scissors, shell, snake, star, and watch.

The key, bell, and snake exports received a local luminance-mask cleanup to remove baked preview checkerboards while preserving their monochrome subjects. All web assets were resized to a maximum of 900 pixels and converted to WebP with alpha where available.
