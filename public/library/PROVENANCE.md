# Library archival backgrounds — provenance

These images are decorative research assets for the personal, noncommercial DIVINE prototype. Source-quality research files are preserved in `assets/library-source/`; the WebP files in `public/library/backgrounds/` are resized and, where noted, cropped derivatives. Rights notes record the best status stated by the holding institution or source site as of September 3, 2026; they are not legal advice.
