# DIVINE generated-art prompt set

All images were generated with the built-in image generator in `stylized-concept` mode for the catalog covers and `ads-marketing` mode for the social preview.

## Shared cover direction

Create a vertical editorial catalog cover for the DIVINE divination series in an exact 4:5 portrait composition. Use a deep matte-black field, dramatic negative space, strictly monochrome black, bone-white, graphite, and cool silver. Combine analog darkroom photography with meticulous fine-art copperplate engraving, tactile photographic grain, soft chemical bloom, precise etched linework, and restrained imperfections. The result should feel like a refined museum-editorial artifact: archival, austere, and mysterious rather than fantasy kitsch. No people, hands, figures, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, fake scripts, or legible writing.

System subjects:

- Tarot: an elegant fan of seven overlapping celestial cards with purely pictorial moon phases, eclipse, stars, rays, and geometric borders.
- Oracle: an antique oval black scrying mirror with concentric liquid ripples emerging from its obsidian surface.
- Lenormand: a long antique iron key arranged like a specimen plate with clover, thorned stem, seed pods, and a moth wing.
- Spellcraft: a single bone-white taper candle, pooled wax, and one dark ritual thread ending in a simple knot; no runes or sigils.
- Ancient Egypt: respectful archaeological plate imagery: dark limestone relief, plain sun disk, stepped horizon geometry, and lotus/papyrus border forms; no hieroglyphs, cartouches, figures, or deities.
- Zodiac: a total solar eclipse with a thin corona, concentric orbital ellipses, unlabeled planetary dots, arcs, and sparse star points; no zodiac glyphs.
- Magic 8 Ball: an abstract monumental obsidian divination sphere on a stone plinth with a triangular aperture containing an ambiguous silver cloud; no numeral 8 and no branded-toy styling.
- Fortune Cookie: a sculptural cookie cracked into two halves with a completely blank curling paper slip and a sparse constellation of crumbs.

## Social-preview direction

Create a landscape social-preview image for the DIVINE divination app. Use a near-black field, immense negative space, subtle warm-white falloff, graphite depth, tactile darkroom grain, thin silver celestial orbital lines, and high-fashion editorial art direction. Center an enormous Bodoni-like title with restrained supporting copy below. Render exactly `DIVINE` and `EIGHT INSTRUMENTS FOR THE UNKNOWN`, both in all caps, with no other text, people, icons, UI, logos, or watermark.
